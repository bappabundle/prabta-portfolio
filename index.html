02 About me

I think before
I design.

I didn't become a designer because I loved Adobe Illustrator. I became a designer because I've always been fascinated by how thoughtful visuals can make information clearer, ideas easier to understand, and communication more effective.

My journey started with simple posters for my local youth organization. Later I learned logo design and digital illustration, and eventually turned commissioned caricatures into one of my first creative businesses.

As my career evolved, I realized design alone was never enough. Working in marketing taught me to ask different questions: why are we creating this, who is this for, what action should people take after seeing it?

Today I approach projects from both creative and business perspectives. Whether it's branding, campaigns, annual reports, or digital content, I believe design should serve a purpose, not just aesthetics.

01

TRAC × RANS
Campaign

Campaign KOL Brand Collaboration

The context

TRAC Bus was promoting its luxury bus offering. I was part of a three-person team handling the collaboration between TRAC and Raffi Ahmad / RANS, during a period when Raffi Ahmad was taking a break from television. There was internal concern from the BOD that he might not match the intended premium-travel niche.

My role

  • Campaign concept & strategy
  • KOL & partnership coordination
  • Host for the media road test
  • Creative direction, asset & content production
  • Team & vendor management

The result

Once the campaign aired, it challenged the internal doubt directly: the luxury bus became fully booked, with verified inquiries from women's/socialite communities interested in booking it for group trips — including arisan trips from Jakarta to Bandung. The audience discovered the service through the Raffi Ahmad campaign.

Lesson learned

A campaign doesn't need to look perfectly targeted on paper to work. What matters is understanding how the audience actually responds — not how a brief predicts they will.

I was also asked to MC a luxury bus road-test event for KOLs in the relevant travel niche.
